## Authorization Rules

1. GitHub usernames must match the account's exact username, not its display name.
2. Only accounts explicitly listed in this section are considered authorized contributors.
3. The Repository Owner is always authorized and must be excluded from automated restriction checks.
4. Accounts not listed in this section are considered unauthorized.
5. Authorization cannot be transferred to another GitHub account or individual.
6. Removing an account from this list immediately revokes its contributor authorization, subject to the applicable terms of the license.
